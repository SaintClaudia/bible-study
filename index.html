Features

Guide

Guidance for the questions that don't always get answered in class — sponsors, annulments, Easter Vigil, and more.

Formation

Short lessons on the Creed, sacraments, prayer, and the faith — plus a full Order of Mass guide and Church Mode for following along in the pew.

Readings

This week's Mass readings at a glance. Dive deeper into Scripture, themes, and context whenever you're ready.

Discover

A curated selection of things to watch, read, and explore — trusted picks to help you grow in the faith.

Listen

Sacred music for prayer, study, and quiet reflection — curated playlists with a built-in player that keeps the music going as you move through the app.

Privacy

Bible Study does not collect any personal information. The app runs entirely in your browser. Any progress you save is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us. We use Google Fonts to serve typefaces. Daily readings are fetched from a free open-source API — no personal data is sent as part of these requests.
