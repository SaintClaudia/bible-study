Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Bible Study is a Catholic pocket companion designed to support your faith journey. We take your privacy seriously.

What we collect

Bible Study does not collect any personal information. The app runs entirely in your browser. Any progress or reflections you save are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts to serve typefaces. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details on how they handle data.

Readings data

Daily Mass readings are fetched from a free, open-source API. No personal data is sent as part of these requests.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out at hello@biblestudylovesyou.com.
