Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Bible Study is a Catholic pocket companion designed to support your faith journey. We are committed to being transparent about what data we collect and why.

What we collect

Bible Study collects no personal information. The app runs in your browser, and any progress you save (such as completed lessons) is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us.

Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate — for example, which sections are visited most. This data is anonymized and does not identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics by using a browser extension such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Third-party services

Bible Study uses the following third-party services:

Google Fonts — used to serve typefaces. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics — used for anonymous usage analytics. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Readings data

Daily Mass readings are fetched from a free, open-source API. No personal data is sent as part of these requests.

Children's privacy

Bible Study is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out at hello@biblestudylovesyou.com.
